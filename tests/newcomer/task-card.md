# Your task

**Make something move.** You have 15 minutes.

On the left is a file, `sketch.roci`, with one line in it. On the right is the window that line draws. Change the file and save it (Ctrl+S), and the window changes straight away.

Anything that moves counts. Some ideas, if you want one:

- a circle that follows the mouse;
- a ball that crosses the window and comes back;
- stars that twinkle;
- a shape that grows when you click.

The page "rocinante on one page" shows the words you can use. If something goes wrong, the window and the terminal say what happened and show a line that would work.

Please think aloud: say what you are trying, what you expect, and what surprises you. There are no wrong answers. We are testing the language, not you.
