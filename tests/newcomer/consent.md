# Taking part in the rocinante newcomer test

rocinante is a small programming language for making pictures and animations. We want to learn whether people who have never seen it can read it and use it. This session tests the language, not you.

**What happens.** About 30 minutes: you read three short programs and say what you think they do (5 minutes), then you try to make something move on the screen (15 minutes), then we talk about how it went (5 minutes). Please think aloud while you work.

**What is kept.** Notes of what you say and do, and the sketch you make. With your permission, also a recording of the screen and your voice, used only to check the notes and deleted once the results are written up. Results are written up without your name: you are a number (P1, P2, ...), with only your rough programming background and first language.

**Your choice.** You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and ask for your notes to be deleted.
