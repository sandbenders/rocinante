2 │ rect at 0 0 size 10 10 fill colors[1]
  │                             ^^^^^^^^^

Square brackets write lists and blocks, so `colors[1]` is not how to read an item. Say which item and of what: the first item is number 1.

Write:  rect at 0 0 size 10 10 fill (item 1 of colors)
