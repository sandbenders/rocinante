── `title` does not go with a sound ─────────────────── sketch.roci:2:10

2 │ set rain title "storm"
  │          ^^^^^^^^^^^^^

`set` takes `title` when it is given a window. With a sound its words are at, volume, pan, speed, loop, note, pitch, attack, decay, sustain, release, bend and vibrato.

Without it:  set rain
