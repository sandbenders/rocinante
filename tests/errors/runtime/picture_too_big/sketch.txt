`pixels` gives every color as an item of a list, and a picture of 1100000 pixels would make a list of that many. Up to a million are listed. Make the picture smaller first, and the sketch carries on with an empty list.

Make it smaller first:
    small = resize photo size 64 0
    colors = pixels small
