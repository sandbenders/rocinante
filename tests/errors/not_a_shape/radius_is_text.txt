This is a record with pos and radius. Its `radius` is text, and has to be a number. A shape is a vector, which is a point, or a record with `pos` and `radius` for a circle, `pos` and `size` for a rectangle, with `angle` to turn it, `from` and `to` for a line, or `points` for any outline.

For example:  ball = { pos: { 200, 100 }, radius: 10 }
