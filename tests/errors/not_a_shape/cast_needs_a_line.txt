This is a record with pos and radius. A line to cast is a record made with `from` and `to`. A shape is a vector, which is a point, or a record with `pos` and `radius` for a circle, `pos` and `size` for a rectangle, with `angle` to turn it, `from` and `to` for a line, or `points` for any outline. With three numbers in its places a shape is in space: a vector of 3 is a point, `pos` and `radius` make a sphere, `pos` and `size` a box (turned by `angle`, three numbers: a turn in degrees about each axis), `from` and `to` a line, and `triangles` a mesh, three places for each triangle.

For example:  h = cast { from: { 0, 0 }, to: mouse } walls
