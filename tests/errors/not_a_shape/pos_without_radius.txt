This is a record with pos and vel. It is made with `pos` but neither `radius` nor `size`. A shape is a vector, which is a point, or a record with `pos` and `radius` for a circle, `pos` and `size` for a rectangle, with `angle` to turn it, `from` and `to` for a line, or `points` for any outline.
