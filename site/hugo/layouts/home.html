a language for prototyping, testing and shipping great ideas, with a fast and modern engine under it

rocinante is for making pictures, animations, sounds and interactive pieces with code. The language is small enough to learn in an afternoon; underneath it is an engine of today's graphics, sound and AI technology. Here's our hello world! One line makes a picture in its own window:

circle at 100 100 radius 50

Save the file and the window changes while it runs. Mistakes never stop it: the window keeps the last good picture and says, in plain words, what went wrong and what would work.

what is under it

graphics
Vulkan, Metal and Direct3D 12, through SDL3 and its GPU API. Shapes are batched and drawn on the GPU: the engine draws 100,000 circles a frame at 60 frames a second.
everywhere
macOS, Windows, Linux and FreeBSD today, with the same sketch behaving the same on each; iPhone and Android apps made from the same sketch are coming.
live
While you work, sketches are interpreted and reload the moment you save, keeping their values. For release they are compiled to native code; that part is coming.
open
Free software under the Zero-Clause BSD licence, the most permissive there is, written in Odin.

guides

Learn it a topic at a time: shapes, movement, input, lists, collision, windows, the turtle, verbs of your own.

reference

Every word of the language, each with an example and the picture it draws.

messages

Everything rocinante can tell you when something is wrong, with a sketch that makes each message.

start

  1. roci new my-sketch

    makes a sketch folder with one circle in it

  2. cd my-sketch
  3. roci run

    opens the window; change sketch.roci and save, and rocinante reloads it while it runs
